Every callout type
Note

Plain hairline. The baseline every other signature is read against.

Info

Same family as Note, same frame.

Todo

Blue, and still a statement rather than advice.

Abstract

Also summary and tldr.

Tip

Advice: the frame plus a solid bar on the leading edge.

Important

Advice too, so the same bar.

Success

A rule across the top rather than down the side — the stamp on a finished thing.

Question

Dashed: the box is not closed because the question is not answered.

Warning

A hazard bar on the leading edge, striped rather than solid.

Failure

Also fail and missing. The heavy frame.

Danger

Also error. Heavy frame, no bar.

Bug

Heavy frame, red.

Example

Dotted: an illustration rather than an instruction.

Quote

No box at all. A quotation is a rule, not a container.
